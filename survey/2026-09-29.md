# Market survey: Dell Precision 5680 / 5690, 2026-09-29

Manual survey done by three agents (eBay EU, refurbished retailers, classifieds) before
any code was written. It is the first labelled dataset for the extractor and the scorer.

**Filters applied:** Precision 5680 or 5690. At least 32 GB RAM. GPU RTX 2000 Ada or
better (A1000, RTX 1000 Ada and integrated graphics are excluded). Keyboard layout ignored.

**Prices** are landed in France, VAT included, for a private buyer. Exchange rate is the
ECB reference for 2026-09-29, 1 GBP = 1.16662 EUR. For UK items, import VAT is estimated
at 20% of price plus shipping; customs duty is 0% on laptops (tariff line 8471.30).

## Reference points (not deals)

- **Retail refurbished:** Cybist (UK) is the only retailer with stock, at €2,371–4,097
  landed plus shipping quoted on request. Amazon.fr has one used 5680 i9 with RTX 3500 Ada
  at €3,200. Other EU and UK refurbishers (EuroPC, Silicon Connect, LapStore, Wisetek,
  Refurbed and others) carried these models but are sold out.
- **New:** high-end-systems (Berlin, Dell partner, eBay DE) sells built-to-order units at
  €3,274–6,364 with a 3-year Dell warranty. This is the price ceiling.
- **Dell:** no refurbished store sells these models to France. The German outlet has
  stopped selling refurbished products, and dellrefurbished.co.uk only goes up to the
  5570 and delivers only in the UK.

## Market read

| Config tier | Used / refurbished, landed FR |
|---|---|
| 32 GB, RTX 2000 Ada | €1,100–1,350 |
| 32–64 GB, RTX 3500 Ada, often OLED + i9 | €2,000–2,250 on eBay (possibly about €1,450 on Leboncoin) |
| New, any config | from about €3,300 |

- **Supply is thin.** Across the whole EU plus UK, only about 10 used units passed the
  filters on this day.
- **Watch for fraud.** Any 32 GB / RTX 2000 Ada claim under about €900 needs proof
  (a Device Manager or dxdiag screenshot) before you pay or travel.
